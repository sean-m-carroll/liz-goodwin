# Dr. Liz Goodwin, OBE

Welcome to Liz’s new website. If you have found this website, you have probably heard of me or met me. If you haven’t done so, let me introduce myself.

My working life has been spent almost entirely focusing on environmental issues and sustainability. For the last 10 years, in particular, I have concentrated my efforts on food waste.

This website will give you an overview of my experience, some of my recent publications and my contact details if you want to get in touch with me.
