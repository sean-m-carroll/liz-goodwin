# Publications

Liz has been instrumental over the past 20+ years in driving food loss and waste avoidance at a global scale. Set out in the following are a few pieces which highlight her knowledge, her insight and leadership.

## Food waste ~Selected interviews, podcasts and commentaries 

**An interview Liz did when she first joined WRI which gave a general overview of the issue of food loss and waste based on her work on food waste in the UK for more than 10 years and having led WRAP as CEO for the previous 9 years.**
https://www.wri.org/insights/qa-liz-goodwin-how-reduce-food-loss-and-waste

**WRI interview with Liz on lessons learnt from reducing food waste in the UK (2016)**
https://www.wri.org/insights/podcast-liz-goodwin-reducing-food-loss-and-waste-lessons-uk

**Commentary by Liz which covered the target, measure, act approach to tackling food loss and waste.** It is a simple strategy which was developed by Champions 12.3 under her watch, and which has now been widely adopted globally.
https://www.wri.org/insights/food-we-dont-eat

**Liz’s commentary during pumpkin season on the importance of reducing pumpkin waste (2016).** This was one of a number of pieces she wrote to pick up on a seasonal moment when food waste might occur to talk about ways in which to avoid wasting food (in this case pumpkins) and remind the reader of the importance of doing so.
https://www.wri.org/insights/dont-let-pumpkin-season-go-waste

**Liz’s commentary on the importance of reducing food waste at Thanksgiving (2016).** This was one of a number of pieces she wrote, to pick up on a seasonal moment when food waste might occur, to talk about ways in which to avoid wasting food (in this case, Thanksgiving) and remind the reader of the importance of doing so.
 https://www.wri.org/insights/5-ways-have-sustainable-thanksgiving

**Liz commentary on the importance of customer loyalty as a driver for businesses to tackling food loss and waste (2017).** There are a whole range of reasons for businesses to tackle food loss and waste – customer loyalty and brand image are increasingly important in a highly competitive retail and food sector.
https://www.wri.org/insights/can-reducing-food-waste-solve-customer-loyalty-problem

**Liz’s commentary on the business case for tackling food loss and waste (2017).** There is a clear financial case for businesses to take action to reduce food loss and waste. This is often not fully realised by businesses who are constantly dealing with many issues and competing agendas. Once a business starts to measure the amount of food that is lost or wasted within their business operations, it quickly rises up the agenda so that the business can realise the financial benefits that result.
https://www.wri.org/insights/numbers-business-case-reducing-food-loss-and-waste

**Liz’s commentary on the importance of valuing our food (2017).** The issue food loss and waste is complex with many drivers and factors at play. There is significant evidence that one of the reasons we, as consumers, waste food is that we have lost ‘our connection’ with food – and we no longer see on a daily basis the work, resources and labour that goes into producing our food. As a result, it has become a commodity product that is always going to be available and so there is no need to protect and preserve what we have. This article explores some of those issues.
https://www.wri.org/insights/appreciating-food-stop-wasting-it

**Liz’s commentary on whether we can really halve food waste and how we might do that (2017).** This article discusses what really needs to happen if we are to achieve a halving of food loss and waste – who needs to take action and the sorts of things that need to change.
https://www.wri.org/insights/can-we-really-cut-food-waste-half

**Liz’s insights commentary on collective responsibility for tackling food loss and waste (2017).** This article discussed the complex interrelated drivers for food loss and waste and how no one actor can deliver real, significant, sustained reductions in isolation. We need collaborative approaches and we all need to play our part – from governments, businesses, farmers, consumers, NGOs and others.
https://www.wri.org/insights/we-have-collective-responsibility-halve-food-loss-and-waste

**Liz’s commentary welcoming Ikea setting a target of halving food waste (2017).** Ikea was the first major company to achieve a 50% reduction in food waste. 
https://www.wri.org/insights/another-step-towards-global-food-loss-and-waste-movement

**Liz’s commentary on the need to increase awareness of food loss and waste (2018).** The issue of food loss and waste is still not recognised by many people and it is still difficult to keep it high on the agenda. 
https://www.wri.org/insights/weve-woken-plastic-waste-food-waste-next

**Liz’s commentary on technologies to help reduce food loss and waste (2019).** There are a growing range of technologies which can help reduce or divert food from being lost or wasted. 
https://www.wri.org/insights/monster-avocados-and-bread-beer-12-technologies-fighting-food-waste

**Liz’s commentary on tips to help keep your food fresh for longer during lockdown (2020).** Finding ways to help keep food fresh is important as a method to reduce food waste at all times but is particularly important at times of restrictions or shortages.
https://www.wri.org/insights/7-tips-make-your-food-last-while-you-stayathome

**Liz’s commentary on importance of tackling food loss and waste and the link with food security (2020).** Tackling food loss and waste is an important part of helping to improve the sustainability of the food system. 
https://www.wri.org/insights/covid-19-has-sparked-food-loss-crisis-here-are-3-ways-tackle-it

**Liz’s commentary on the food systems summit in 2021, co-authored with Craig Hanson, Edward Davey, Rebecca Carter, Sophie Wood and Janet Ranganathan (2021).** The food systems summit in 2021 was a significant opportunity to discuss and make progress in moving towards a more sustainable food system. 
https://www.wri.org/insights/un-food-summit-can-improve-food-systems

**Liz’s commentary on the challenges of ending food waste (Podcast – How do we end food waste?) (2021).** This podcast talked about the issue of food waste and progress being made to reduce it as well as discussing what more is needed.
https://www.wri.org/podcasts/how-do-we-end-food-loss-and-waste

**Liz’s commentary on shifting social norms to make food waste more socially unacceptable, co-authored with Stacy Blondin and Sophie Attwood (2022).** This article covered the behavioural factors that contribute to food waste and which have resulted in wasting food seem a fairly normal thing to do. It also considers how these might be addressed – to drive behavioural change and move towards a society where wasting food is no longer the norm.
https://www.wri.org/insights/shifting-social-norms-food-waste

**Liz’s commentary on the size of the food loss and waste problem and the value of measurement, co-authored with Brian Lipinski (2024).** This article reviewed the issue or food loss and waste, the size of the problem and the importance of measurement. Measurement is needed in order to understand where hot spots of loss or waste exist, which will then allow targeted action to address those hot spots and deliver change.
https://www.wri.org/insights/how-much-food-does-the-world-waste

**Liz’s commentary on from 2025 on the subject of food loss and waste – why it’s important, what causes it and what can be done about it (2025).** This article gives an update on understanding of food loss and waste, the impacts of the loss and waste, the drivers causing it to happen and what actions are required to reduce it.
https://www.wri.org/insights/reducing-food-loss-and-food-waste

**Liz’s commentary on the importance of tackling food loss and waste in a world facing food security issues and tariffs (2025).** This article discusses the role of food loss and waste in a world where a number of global issues are centre-stage, including wars, economic crises and cost of living pressures. These global issues provide a backdrop and also a reason to increase the focus on food loss and waste as tackling it has an important part to play in managing these complex issues which are largely outside the control of most people.
https://www.wri.org/insights/wasting-less-food-resilient-tariff-fix
