
# Overview

## Focus

## Profile

An inspirational senior leader with in-depth experience of public, private, charity / third sectors. A clear communicator, strategist, planner, analytical thinker who is delivery and results focused. Wide ranging media and public speaking experience. Developing successful stakeholder relationships including government ministers, senior government officers, industry leaders and NGOs. Extensive overseas experience in both global north and south.
